Public health

Number of measles cases this year

2,566

Spread across at least 38 active outbreaks nationwide, including two deaths in Pennsylvania — the first measles deaths recorded in 2026.

From the source

CIDRAP reports the 2026 US measles total has climbed past 2,500 cases, driven by outbreaks concentrated in Pennsylvania, Ohio, Utah, Washington, Arizona, and Virginia, among other states, with 38 distinct outbreaks contributing to the total.

— CIDRAP, University of Minnesota, "With 101 new cases, US measles total tops 2,500"

Methodology. This is a cumulative year-to-date total, not a daily average — it's the most recently reported running count for 2026, sourced from an independent, non-government tracker rather than the CDC directly, per how this site's tiles are meant to be sourced.

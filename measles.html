Public health

Number of deaths from measles since Trump took office in January 2025

5

Total number of measles cases since January 2025

5,423

Measles was officially declared eliminated in the United States in 2000. The US is now at risk of losing its official elimination status.

From the source

Three people died from measles in 2025 — two unvaccinated children in the Texas outbreak, and one unvaccinated adult in New Mexico. Pennsylvania health officials confirmed two more deaths in Lancaster County on August 20, 2026, both unvaccinated — the state's first measles fatalities in 35 years, and the first recorded in the US in 2026. "We want to ensure every Pennsylvanian has the information they need to protect themselves, their loved ones and their communities," said Pennsylvania Health Secretary Debra Bogen.

— Axios, "Deadly measles surge: U.S. reports first 2 deaths of 2026" (August 25, 2026); Scientific American, "The U.S. Just Recorded Its First Two Deaths From Measles for 2026"

Elimination status

Measles was declared eliminated from the United States in 2000, meaning the disease had no continuous spread here for over a year. Sustained outbreaks since have put that status "in jeopardy," pending a review of transmission data by the Pan American Health Organization later in 2026.

— CDC, Measles History; CIDRAP, "Measles cases reach 35-year high in US"

Methodology. Both figures are cumulative since January 2025. The case count (5,423) adds the CDC's final 2025 tally (2,289 cases, the most since 1991) to its running 2026 tally (3,134 cases as of September 3, 2026). The death count (5) combines the 3 deaths reported in 2025 (Texas and New Mexico) with the 2 reported so far in 2026 (Pennsylvania) — drawn from news reporting rather than the CDC directly, since the CDC's own death count is based on national death-certificate records (NCHS), which take months to process and had not caught up to either outbreak as of this writing.

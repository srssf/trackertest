Public health

Number of deaths from measles this year

2

Number of measles cases this year: 3,134.

Measles was officially declared eliminated in the United States in 2000. The US is now at risk of losing its official elimination status.

From the source

Pennsylvania health officials confirmed two measles deaths in Lancaster County on August 20, 2026 — the state's first measles fatalities in 35 years, and the first recorded in the US in 2026. Both individuals were unvaccinated. "We want to ensure every Pennsylvanian has the information they need to protect themselves, their loved ones and their communities," said Pennsylvania Health Secretary Debra Bogen.

— Scientific American, "The U.S. Just Recorded Its First Two Deaths From Measles for 2026" (August 25, 2026)

Elimination status

Measles was declared eliminated from the United States in 2000, meaning the disease had no continuous spread here for over a year. Sustained outbreaks since have put that status "in jeopardy," pending a review of transmission data by the Pan American Health Organization later in 2026.

— CDC, Measles History; CIDRAP, "Measles cases reach 35-year high in US"

Methodology. Both figures are cumulative year-to-date totals, not daily averages. The case count (3,134) comes directly from the CDC, which publishes the most current national tally. The death count (2) comes from Pennsylvania's own health department announcement, reported by Scientific American and CIDRAP — the CDC's own death count still showed zero as of this writing, since its figure is based on national death-certificate records (NCHS), which take months to process and had not yet caught up to the outbreak.

Cost of war

Today the Iran War cost you an average of

$3.80

Total cost to date: $40 billion+ nationally in extra fuel costs alone (as of the most recent research brief).

From the source

The tracker measures "the extra cost paid by U.S. consumers for gasoline and diesel since the Iran conflict began on February 28, 2026," comparing actual retail fuel prices against a modeled baseline of what prices would have been without the conflict, then multiplying the daily price gap by national fuel consumption data.

— Watson Institute for International and Public Affairs, Brown University, Iran War Energy Cost Tracker

Additional sources

  • Costs of War Project, Brown University

    The Watson Institute's longer-running project on the full human and financial costs of post-9/11 U.S. wars, now tracking Iran alongside Iraq, Afghanistan, and others.

  • Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy: Iran War Fuel Costs

    An independent analysis of how the conflict's fuel-price effects are distributed across income levels.

  • National Priorities Project: How Much Is the War in Iran Costing Taxpayers?

    A fact sheet breaking down direct military spending on top of the consumer fuel-cost impact.

Methodology. The per-day average divides the Watson Institute's latest reported per-household total by the number of days since the conflict began on February 28, 2026. Because the live tracker updates continuously with fuel-price data but isn't machine-readable from here, the total shown reflects the most recent published research brief rather than a real-time figure.

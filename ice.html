Detention & deportation

Today ICE booked into detention approximately

~1,050 human beings

Of whom an estimated ~226 are children on an average day. Total number of people currently in ICE custody: 60,311.

From the source

TRAC-based analysis finds ICE interior enforcement book-ins running at roughly 955 per day and CBP book-ins at about 94 per day as of March 2026, while the total detained population peaked at a record 70,766 on January 24, 2026 before declining to 60,311 by April 4, 2026. Separately, reporting on children specifically finds "over 6,200 children have been held by ICE" since Trump's second term began, with the daily average of detained children rising from 24 under the prior administration's final year to about 226 per day — peaking above 550 in January 2026.

— TRAC, Syracuse University, Immigration Detention Quick Facts; The Marshall Project, "Trump Has Put 500 Babies and Toddlers In Immigration Jails"

Methodology. The book-in average combines the most recently published ICE interior enforcement and CBP daily rates (March 2026). The children figure is the Marshall Project's own calculated daily average of children detained since the start of the current administration. The total-in-custody figure is TRAC's most recent published snapshot; none of these sources currently publish a literal same-day count, so all figures here are the latest available rather than real-time.

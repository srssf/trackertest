Losing coverage

Average number of Americans losing health care every day

~13,700

Based on an actual, already-occurred decline — not a future projection.

From the source

"National Medicaid enrollment declined by 5 million people" between April 2025 and April 2026, a 6% decrease, according to KFF's analysis of CMS enrollment data. The tracker notes that "April 2026 data are preliminary and are subject to change in subsequent enrollment reports."

— KFF, Medicaid/CHIP Enrollment Tracker

Additional sources

  • Center on Budget and Policy Priorities: By the Numbers

    Summarizes CBO's separate projection that the 2025 tax-and-spending law's Medicaid and ACA marketplace cuts will push roughly 15 million more people onto the uninsured rolls by 2034 — on top of the decline already measured above.

  • Georgetown Center for Children and Families: Medicaid, CHIP, and ACA Cuts, Explained

    A plain-language breakdown of exactly which provisions in the law are driving coverage losses.

  • KFF: Medicaid — What to Watch in 2026

    Ongoing tracking of work-requirement rollouts and other policy changes likely to accelerate coverage loss.

Methodology. This figure divides KFF's measured 5 million-person Medicaid/CHIP enrollment decline (April 2025 to April 2026) by 365 days. Worth knowing: this decline has more than one cause — including post-pandemic "unwinding" and new work requirements — not solely the law's tax-cut-funded cuts, though those cuts are a documented driver both KFF and CBO point to.
